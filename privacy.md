# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** July 2026

## Overview

Ecelyo is currently in an early alpha testing phase distributed through Apple's TestFlight.

Your privacy is important. Ecelyo is designed to collect no personal data.

## Information We Collect

Ecelyo does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information.

Specifically, Ecelyo does not:

- Require user accounts
- Require user registration
- Collect names or email addresses
- Collect location data
- Access your contacts
- Access your photos
- Access your camera
- Access your microphone
- Display advertising
- Track your activity
- Use analytics services
- Share information with third parties

## TestFlight

If you install Ecelyo through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may collect diagnostic information such as crash reports according to Apple's own Privacy Policy.

Ecelyo does not receive or process this information unless it is explicitly provided by Apple.

## Third-Party Services

Ecelyo does not integrate any third-party services that collect personal information.

## Children's Privacy

Ecelyo is not directed at children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Ecelyo evolves. Any future changes will be published on this page.
